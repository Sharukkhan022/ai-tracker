AI Progress EngineVersion 2.0 - Final Draft

1. Executive Summary

AI Progress Engine is an AI-powered web application that helps students plan learning, generate AI quizzes, track progress, and receive personalized recommendations.

2. Product Vision

Measure understanding through AI-assisted assessments instead of only tracking study activity.
